Phase 2: the throne is defended

The Golden Knight keeps the throne with the highest series points total, or is usurped by the White Knight, the Black Knight, or an unknown challenger. If no Golden Knight rose in Phase 1, the search continues: the player with the most points after the final tournament is crowned.

Phase 1: the knights rise

Golden Knight

Leads the points total after the first four tournaments and wins the 1v1.

White Knight

If a Golden Knight rises: 2nd in points. If not: the points leader who didn't win the 1v1.

Black Knight

If a Golden Knight rises: 1v1 runner-up (or 3rd in the 1v1 if the runner-up is already the White Knight). If not: the 1v1 champion.

Frequently Asked

How much does it cost?

Coaching is $80/month for every program. Club players may have extra league/tournament and uniform fees (TBD).

Where is training?

At the YWAM Lindale fields in Lindale, TX.

Which group is my child in?

At launch there is one group per program: Soccer Start U3–U5, Academy U6–U10, Knights U11–U14. As groups fill, they split into age groups (U3/U4/U5, U6/U8/U10, U11/U13). Not sure? Ask us below.

Do parents stay during Soccer Start?

Yes, a parent stays on site for Soccer Start.

Are boys and girls together?

Soccer Start is coed. The Academy starts coed and splits boys and girls onto separate fields once groups are big enough.

What should my player bring?

A ball, water, shin guards, and cleats or turf shoes.
